# CERTIFY.CX — Marketing Funnel & Automation
## Client Guide & System Overview

This guide explains how the system you requested now works — from the Arabic
marketing ad all the way to the issued certificate — and how the automated
emails and notifications keep everyone informed at each step.

---

## 1. What You Requested

You asked for two things:

1. **An Arabic marketing funnel** — a link for Facebook & Instagram ads that
   takes a customer to an ISO certificate page in Arabic, where they do a basic
   registration (name & email), then go to the payment gateway and pay (Credit/
   Debit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, or MoneyGram) to start their certification.

2. **Email automation & notifications** — automatic emails to customers from
   **support@certify.cx** throughout the certification process.

Below is how each part works now.

---

## 2. The Customer Journey (Step by Step)

### Step 1 — The Arabic Ad Link
The customer clicks your ad link, for example:

> **https://certify.cx/iso/iso-9001?lang=ar**

The `?lang=ar` part guarantees the page opens **directly in Arabic**, no matter
where the customer is located. You can use this for any standard, e.g.
`/iso/iso-27001?lang=ar`, `/iso/iso-14001?lang=ar`.

### Step 2 — The Arabic ISO Page
The ISO certificate page opens **fully in Arabic** with right-to-left layout.
The customer sees the standard, its benefits, the three packages
(**Bronze / Silver / Gold**) with pricing, and a clear "Apply" button.

### Step 3 — Basic Registration
The customer enters their **name and email**, sets a password, and verifies
their email. This is quick and low-friction — designed for ad traffic.

### Step 4 — Payment
The customer is taken to the payment gateway and pays for their chosen package.

### Step 5 — Certification Starts
After payment, the application is created and enters the certification process:
**review → audit → certificate issued.** The customer can track everything from
their dashboard.

---

## 3. Automated Emails (from support@certify.cx)

The customer receives an automatic email at every milestone — your team does not
need to send anything manually:

| When | Email the customer receives |
|------|------------------------------|
| Account created | Verify Email, then Welcome |
| **Payment completed** | **Payment Confirmation** |
| **Application submitted** | **Application Received** |
| **Audit booked** | **Audit Scheduled** |
| **Audit finished** | **Audit Completed** |
| **Certificate ready** | **Certificate Issued** |
| 30 days before expiry | **Renewal Reminder** (fully automatic) |

---

## 4. Admin Notifications (for your team)

Your team is also alerted automatically — both inside the dashboard and by email:

| Event | Your team is notified |
|-------|------------------------|
| New registration / lead | New Registration alert |
| Payment received | Payment Received alert |
| New certification request | New Certification Request alert |

This means nothing slips through — every new customer and payment is visible
immediately.
