# Certify.cx — 60-Second Corporate Introductory Video

**Purpose:** One introductory video to be shared with all stakeholders — clients, referral partners, consultancies, auditors, certification bodies and investors — and posted on LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp.

**Tool:** HeyGen (subscription active, linked to `wcaglobal411@gmail.com`)
**Route in HeyGen:** *Script to Video* (not the Auto-pilot AI box)
**Master format:** 16:9 landscape. A 9:16 portrait cut is also required for Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp Status.

**Length:** 133 words ≈ 55–58 seconds at normal speaking pace.

## Full storyboard

### Scene 1 — 0:00–0:06 · The problem
**Voiceover:** "Getting ISO certified usually means months of emails, unclear costs, and people you never meet."
**On-screen text:** `ISO certification. Slow. Unclear.`
**Visual:** Cluttered desk, stacked paperwork, a clock. Muted / desaturated colours.
**Note:** This is the scroll-stopper. It must land in the first 3 seconds or the viewer scrolls past.

### Scene 2 — 0:06–0:17 · What Certify.cx is
**Voiceover:** "Certify.cx puts it all in one place. Apply online, get matched with an accredited certification body, upload your documents, and track every step to your certificate."
**On-screen text:** `ONE PLATFORM. START TO CERTIFICATE.`
**Visual:** Screen recording of the actual dashboard — application list, status moving forward. Colour turns bright/teal here, contrasting with Scene 1.

### Scene 3 — 0:17–0:28 · Industries *(the most-asked question)*
**Voiceover:** "We serve manufacturing — food and non-food — construction, services, medical, restaurants and hotels, schools and universities, exporters, and farming. ISO applies to every sector, and every size of organisation."
**On-screen text:** Icon montage, one per sector, appearing in time with the words:
`🏭 Manufacturing · 🏗️ Construction · 💼 Services · 🏥 Medical · 🍽️ Restaurants & Hotels · 🎓 Schools & Universities · 📦 Exporters · 🌾 Farming`
**Closing line on screen:** `Every sector. Every size.`
**Visual:** Fast, rhythmic icon montage. Keep it moving — this is a list, and lists get boring if they sit still.

### Scene 4 — 0:28–0:41 · Why we are different
**Voiceover:** "What's different? Transparent pricing up front. One dashboard shared by clients, auditors and certification bodies. Nine languages. And live status — you always know where you stand."
**On-screen text:** Checkmarks appearing one at a time:
`✓ Transparent pricing`
`✓ One shared dashboard`
`✓ Nine languages`
`✓ Live tracking`
**Visual:** Clean white background, teal checkmarks animating in.

### Scene 5 — 0:41–0:53 · What each stakeholder gets
**Voiceover:** "Clients certify faster. Referral partners and consultancies earn commission on every client they bring. Auditors and certification bodies receive verified, ready work."
**On-screen text:** Five labels appearing in a row:
`CLIENTS · REFERRALS · CONSULTANCIES · AUDITORS · CERTIFICATION BODIES`
**Visual:** Five icons across the screen, each lighting up as it is mentioned.
**Note:** This is the scene that makes one video work for six audiences. Do not cut it for time.

### Scene 6 — 0:53–1:00 · Call to action
**Voiceover:** "Certify.cx. Certification, made clear. Register free today at certify dot c x."
**On-screen text:** `certify.cx` — large, with the logo above it.
**Visual:** Full teal background (`#0e9f6e`), white logo and wordmark. Hold this frame for the last 2 seconds so it is the freeze-frame on social platforms.

# ⚠️ Confirm with the client BEFORE publishing

Once this is on social media it cannot be taken back. Three claims need a yes:

1. **"Register free"** — registration is free; the certification package is paid. Confirm this wording is how the client wants it framed.
2. **"Nine languages"** — factually correct (English, Spanish, Arabic, German, French, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese). Confirm the client wants it stated.
3. **"Transparent pricing up front"** — depends on the pricing model question still open from 19 July (single US$999 covering 3 years, versus "1 Year Initial" plus US$150/year). Settle that before the video says "transparent pricing".

Also confirm: **is an Arabic version required?**
